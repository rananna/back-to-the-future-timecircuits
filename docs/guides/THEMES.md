# 🎨 Web Interface Themes Guide

The Time Circuits clock includes several built-in themes to customize the look and feel of the web interface. This feature is purely cosmetic and does not affect the behavior of the physical display. It allows you to personalize your experience while configuring the clock from your browser.

---

### How to Change the Theme

1.  **Navigate to the Web Interface**: Open your web browser and go to your clock's IP address or `http://bttf-clock.local`.
2.  **Open the Theme Selector**: At the bottom of the navigation menu on the left side of the page, you will find the theme selector.
3.  **Choose a Theme**: Click on any of the theme options to instantly apply it. The web interface will update with the new color scheme, and your selection will be saved automatically.

### Available Themes

Here is a list of the available themes and a brief description of their color schemes, inspired by iconic elements from the *Back to the Future* trilogy.

*   **Time Circuits (Default)**
    *   **Colors**: Bright greens and cyans on a dark background.
    *   **Inspiration**: The classic, default look of the web interface, matching the project's primary branding.

*   **OUTATIME**
    *   **Colors**: Bold reds and yellows.
    *   **Inspiration**: The iconic California "OUTATIME" license plate on the DeLorean.

*   **88 MPH**
    *   **Colors**: Cool blues and teals.
    *   **Inspiration**: The blue flashes of light and sparks generated as the DeLorean hits 88 miles per hour.

*   **Plutonium Glow**
    *   **Colors**: Soft, glowing greens and yellows.
    *   **Inspiration**: The radioactive glow of the plutonium that powers the time machine in 1985.

*   **Mr. Fusion**
    *   **Colors**: Vibrant oranges and grays.
    *   **Inspiration**: The orange and silver design of the Mr. Fusion Home Energy Reactor that powers the time circuits in the future.

*   **Clock Tower**
    *   **Colors**: Antique golds and beiges.
    *   **Inspiration**: The colors of the Hill Valley Courthouse clock tower, which famously gets struck by lightning to send Marty back to 1985.
