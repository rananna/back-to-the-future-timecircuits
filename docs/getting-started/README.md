# Getting Started Guide

Welcome to the Time Circuits project! This guide provides a complete, step-by-step path to building your own replica. We'll walk you through purchasing components, assembling the hardware, and installing the firmware.

## The 3 Steps to Success

Building your Time Circuits clock involves three main stages. Follow them in order to ensure a smooth setup process.

### **Step 1: The Build**
This is the hardware phase, where you'll gather your components and assemble the physical device.

*   **[Bill of Materials (BOM)](./bom.md)**: A complete list of all the electronic components you'll need to purchase.
*   **[Pinout Reference](./pinout-reference.md)**: The official wiring diagram and pinout for connecting the components to your ESP32.
*   **[3D Printed Case Guide](./3d-case.md)**: Find links to download the 3D printable files and get tips for a successful print.

### **Step 2: The Firmware**
This is the software phase, where you'll flash the brains of the clock.

*   **[Firmware Installation Guide](./firmware-installation.md)**: Detailed, step-by-step instructions for setting up the Arduino IDE, installing libraries, and uploading the firmware and web UI.

### **Step 3: The Setup**
This is the final configuration phase, where you'll connect the clock to your network and learn how to use it.

*   **[First-Time WiFi Setup](./wifi-setup.md)**: Learn how to connect your new clock to your home WiFi network.
*   **[Web Interface Guide](../user-guide/web-interface.md)**: Take a complete tour of the web UI to configure all your clock's settings and features.

---

> 🎉 **Once you've completed these steps, your Time Circuits clock will be fully operational!**
>
> After that, you can explore the **[User Guides](../user-guide/README.md)** to learn about more advanced features, like Home Assistant integration.
